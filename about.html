Rigorous Standards

The Mamun 150-Point Certification

Before entering our showroom floor, every vehicle undergoes a comprehensive 4-stage technical vetting procedure.

Stage 01
Structural & Body Integrity

Laser chassis alignment checks, paint-thickness depth gauge testing, panel gap symmetry, and undercarriage rust inspection.

Stage 02
Engine & Powertrain

Compression testing, fluid leak analysis, turbocharger spool evaluation, transmission shift smooth-ratio check, and exhaust emissions.

Stage 03
Electrical & Diagnostics

Full ECU OBD-II diagnostic scan, battery health test, radar sensor calibration, infotainment checks, and camera matrix analysis.

Stage 04
Road Test & Verification

Comprehensive dynamic road test evaluating suspension travel, ABS braking response, active lane guidance, and genuine mileage audit.
